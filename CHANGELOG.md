# Changelog

## 0.1 (2026-10-15)

- **First release.** See where your disk space goes: a usage bar of the whole disk, plus the biggest folders in
  your home and in ~/Library.
- Choose what to scan: a quick disk overview, the slower search for node_modules and vendor folders, or
  everything. Wombat keeps the last scan and shows it when you open it again.
- A cleanup list with "Safe" items you can clean from the app (Trash, caches, npm cache, Xcode leftovers,
  node_modules) and "Check first" items that open in Finder.
- Pick node_modules and Composer vendor folders project by project.
- Nothing is deleted without a confirmation, and Wombat never connects to the internet.
